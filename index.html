Oplan Kontra Baha

"Oplan Kontra Baha (OKB) is a non-structural flood mitigation initiative of the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) aimed at restoring and maintaining the flow of waterways through desilting, dredging, clearing, and removal of obstructions, in coordination with local government units and other stakeholders. It seeks to reduce the frequency, depth, and duration of flooding by improving the capacity of rivers, creeks, canals, and drainage systems to carry water."
